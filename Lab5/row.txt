Dataset for Practicing Regex Matching

1. Strings with 'a' followed by zero or more 'b's
   a
   ab
   abb
   abbb
   aBBBB
   acb
   bba
   aaabb

2. Strings with 'a' followed by exactly two to three 'b's
   abb
   abbb
   abbbb
   aBBBB
   a
   acb
   aabbb

3. Sequences of lowercase letters joined with an underscore
   hello_world
   regex_practice
   test_case
   Python_Regex
   valid_variable
   not_valid123
   _underscore

4. One uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters
   Apple
   HelloWorld
   RegexPractice
   UPPERCASE
   lowercase
   Test123

5. Strings with 'a' followed by anything, ending in 'b'
   axb
   a123b
   ab
   aXyzZb
   acdb
   bbba
   abc

6. Replace space, comma, or dot with a colon
   Hello, world. How are you?
   This is a test, a simple one.
   Replace spaces, commas, and dots.
   Regex is fun.

7. Convert snake_case to camelCase
   hello_world → helloWorld
   convert_this_example → convertThisExample
   snake_case_string → snakeCaseString
   this_is_a_test → thisIsATest

8. Split a string at uppercase letters
   HelloWorldExample → ["Hello", "World", "Example"]
   ThisIsATest → ["This", "Is", "A", "Test"]
   RegexPracticeTask → ["Regex", "Practice", "Task"]

9. Insert spaces between words starting with capital letters
   HelloWorld → Hello World
   InsertSpacesHere → Insert Spaces Here
   MyNewString → My New String

10. Convert camelCase to snake_case
   helloWorld → hello_world
   convertThisExample → convert_this_example
   camelCaseString → camel_case_string
   thisIsATest → this_is_a_test
